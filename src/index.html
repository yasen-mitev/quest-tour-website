How it plays

Four moves, repeated across the city.

A game is an ordered chain of landmarks. The team cannot skip ahead: solving the riddle in front of them is the only way to the next one.

1 / 4

Solve the riddle

Each task is a short riddle about a place. Wrong answers are tolerated, spelling is forgiven, and the team keeps guessing.

2 / 4

Walk there

The answer is the landmark. Finding it is part of the game: there is no map and no navigation, only the city and your team.

3 / 4

Take a team photo

A photo at every landmark is required. The team never sees it again until the album arrives.

4 / 4

Read the story

Two or three paragraphs about the place, in the team's language, then the next riddle unlocks.

Every cost is shown before it is paid. Hints and giving up on a riddle add minutes to the team's time. The player confirms the price first, and the clock keeps running from Start to the last landmark.

For hosts

Built for the person who runs the day.

A colleague’s workshop week, a friend’s first visit, a family weekend. You write the riddles once, and every team gets the same fair game.

One private link per team

Players open it on their own phones. No accounts, no app store, nothing to explain at the start line.

Several phones, one run

Everyone on the team sees the same state. When one phone solves a riddle, the others move on with a short toast.

Clock and leaderboard on the server

Timings, answers and penalties are checked server-side, and every team of a game lands on one ranking. Nobody can pause, replay or edit a result from a phone.

Hints with a price tag

Two hints and a last-resort reveal, each with a penalty you set per game. Players see the cost before they pay it.

Riddles in every language you add

Translate a game once. Players pick their language from a pill in the corner, and stories, riddles and hints follow.

Every riddle gets rated

Right after a riddle is solved, players tap one to five stars, and at the finish they can leave a word for you. You see the average and the comments per riddle, so the weak ones get rewritten.

The surprise

The photos come back as a memories album.

Players never see their photos during the game, not even a thumbnail. When the run is over, the host sends the team an album: every landmark, the team at it, the time they got there, and the story of the place.

  • A web album at the team's own link, and the same album as a print-ready A4 PDF.
  • The facts of the day on the cover: date, landmarks reached, total time, place.
  • Photos stay at original quality and are deleted when you say so.

Setting up

A quest takes an afternoon to write.

1 / 3

Describe the landmarks

Name, riddle, accepted answers, two hints and the story, with a picture if you like. A landmark is written once and reused in any game.

2 / 3

Order a game, invite teams

Pick the landmarks in order, set the time limit and the penalties, name the teams and share their links. Links open and close on the dates you choose.

3 / 3

Watch the day, send the albums

Follow the leaderboard live, read the teams' feedback, then generate the albums and send the surprise.

Questions

Before you send a team out

Do players need to install anything?
No. The team link opens in the phone's browser, on iOS Safari and Android Chrome. There are no accounts for players, the link is the only way in.
What happens when the signal drops?
A slim banner says the app is reconnecting, and it recovers on its own. Because the clock runs on the server, a dead spot never changes a team's time.
Can a team play the same game twice?
No, a game can never be replayed: reopening the link shows the finish screen again. Hosts get a service link that ignores time limits and can be reset for testing a route.
Who sees the photos?
Only the host, until the album is sent. Photos are stored at original quality and never re-encoded; the host can delete them at any time.
How long does a quest take?
You set the maximum per game. The sample Sofia route is 8 riddles and 4 hours; most teams finish in under 3.
Can I book a quest for my group?
It is not a paid service. We run quests for friends and colleagues who visit Sofia; if you are one of them, your host sends you the team link. Everything else is in the contact section.
