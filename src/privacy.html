What we collect, and why

  • Host accounts: name, email address and password hash, to sign in and to send confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • Game content: landmarks, riddles, team names and the first names of participants, entered by the host to run a game.
  • Game runs: answers, timings, hints and penalties, to run the clock and the leaderboard.
  • Team photos: taken by players at each landmark and kept at original quality for the host's album.
  • Technical logs: IP address and browser, kept briefly to keep the service running and secure.
