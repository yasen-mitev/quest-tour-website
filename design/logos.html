Six marks to choose from.

All six use only the app's colours and are drawn as one SVG each, so the same file works on the patina band, on the light surface and as a favicon. The wordmark stays Onest 800, the same face as every title in the app.

After you pick one

  1. It becomes assets/logo.svg, favicon.svg, a 180 px apple-touch-icon.png and the mark in the OG image.
  2. The nav, the footer and the closing card of the video switch to it; the app's loading mark can follow in a separate PR.
  3. Shortlist suggestion: B (recommended) for recognisability at small sizes, B2 if you prefer the skyline inside the pin, C if you want the hero skyline everywhere, A if nothing should change.
